What NetPulse does

Monitoring-team depth. Desktop-app simplicity.

Built by a network engineer for the people who get called when the Wi-Fi "isn't working." Everything below runs quietly on a single Mac.

At-a-glance verdict

One screen says whether the site is healthy right now — no dashboards to assemble, no metrics to interpret.

Independent internet checks

Continuously reaches Cloudflare (1.1.1.1), Google (8.8.8.8) and Quad9 (9.9.9.9), so you know instantly if it's the ISP or the LAN.

WAN throughput & usage

Measures real bandwidth and tracks usage over time — passively, without saturating a line someone's trying to work on.

Device discovery

Finds what's on the network with ARP and ping — no aggressive port scanning — and remembers each device site by site.

Wi-Fi & backhaul health

Signal and mesh backhaul strength in real dBm, so you catch a weak node before the client does.

Provider status feeds

Pulls official status pages for the services a site depends on — so when it isn't you, you can prove it.

Alerts when it drops

Know the moment a site goes down — on your channels — instead of hearing about it from an angry phone call.

History & timeline

What happened overnight, last week, during the outage — kept and searchable, not lost the moment you look away.

Live network map

See the topology — gateway, nodes and devices — and watch paths go green or red in real time.

One-click client reports

Branded, professional findings you can hand over or email — the deliverable that closes the consult.

Every site, one place

Manage many client sites, each with its own baseline, devices and alert setup — recognized automatically when you arrive.

Quiet & respectful

macOS-native and passive-first. It watches without stressing the network or getting in anyone's way.
